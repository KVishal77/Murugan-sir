Biography & Academic Background

Dr. R. Mayilmurugan received his Ph.D. from Bharathidasan University, Tiruchirapalli, under the supervision of distinguished bioinorganic chemist Prof. M. Palaniandavar, FNA, FASc, and M.Sc. from Madurai Kamaraj University (ANJA College), securing the Gold Medal / First Rank.

He completed Post-Doctoral Fellowships with Prof. Nadia C. Mösch-Zanetti (University of Graz, Austria) focusing on biomimetic chemistry of molybdenum oxotransferases, and with Prof. Janet Morrow (University at Buffalo, SUNY, USA) as a Fulbright Scholar working on iron-based PARACEST MRI contrast agents. He was also an INSA-DFG Visiting Scientist at Heidelberg University, Germany with Prof. Peter Comba. Prior to joining IIT Bhilai, he served as Assistant Professor at Madurai Kamaraj University.
