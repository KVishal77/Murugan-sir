About the Principal Investigator

Academic background, international experience, and research vision

Dr. Ramasamy Mayilmurugan

Associate Professor, IIT Bhilai

Dr. Mayilmurugan earned his Ph.D. from Bharathidasan University under the mentorship of Prof. M. Palaniandavar (FNA, FASc) and his M.Sc. from Madurai Kamaraj University. He expanded his global research expertise through prestigious postdoctoral fellowships:

  • Fulbright Scholar with Prof. Janet Morrow at the University at Buffalo, SUNY, USA.
  • Postdoctoral researcher with Prof. Nadia C. Mösch-Zanetti at the University of Graz, Austria.
  • INSA-DFG Visiting Scientist at Heidelberg University, Germany.

His multidisciplinary group bridges chemistry and biology—addressing critical healthcare and catalytic challenges through rigorous molecular design and synthesis.
