Principal investigator

Dr. Ramasamy Mayilmurugan

Associate Professor, Department of Chemistry & Department of Bioscience and Biomedical Engineering, Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Bhilai
murugan@iitbhilai.ac.in

Google Scholar profile ORCID

Dr. Mayilmurugan received his PhD from Bharathidasan University, Tiruchirappalli, under Prof. M. Palaniandavar, FASc, and his M.Sc. from Madurai Kamaraj University. He performed postdoctoral research with Prof. Nadia C. Mösch-Zanetti at the University of Graz, Austria, and with Prof. Janet Morrow at UB SUNY as a Fulbright scholar.
