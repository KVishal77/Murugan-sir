About the Principal Investigator

Academic background, international experience, and research leadership

Google Scholar ORCID ID

Dr. Ramasamy Mayilmurugan

Associate Professor, Department of Chemistry, IIT Bhilai

Dr. R. Mayilmurugan received his Ph.D. from Bharathidasan University, Tiruchirapalli, under the supervision of distinguished bioinorganic chemist Prof. M. Palaniandavar, FNA, FASc[cite: 3]. He earned his M.Sc. from Madurai Kamaraj University (ANJA College)[cite: 3], where he was awarded a gold medal for securing the first rank[cite: 3]. Before joining IIT Bhilai, he served as an Assistant Professor at Madurai Kamaraj University[cite: 3].

His global research exposure includes prestigious post-doctoral and visiting fellowships:

  • Fulbright Scholar Award (2014)[cite: 3]: Worked on iron-based PARACEST MRI contrast agents in Prof. Janet Morrow's group at the University at Buffalo, SUNY, USA[cite: 3].
  • Post-Doctoral Fellowship: Focused on biomimetic chemistry of molybdenum-containing oxo transferase enzymes and reaction mechanisms with Prof. Nadia C. Mösch-Zanetti at the University of Graz, Austria[cite: 3].
  • INSA-DFG Visiting Scientist: Conducted research at Prof. Peter Comba's laboratory at Heidelberg University, Germany[cite: 3].
