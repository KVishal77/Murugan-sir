About Principal Investigator

Dr. Ramasamy Mayilmurugan

Associate Professor, Department of Chemistry

Indian Institute of Technology (IIT), Bhilai

Email: murugan@iitbhilai.ac.in

Google Scholar Profile ORCID Profile

Dr. R. Mayilmurugan received his PhD from the Bharathidasan University, Tiruchirapalli, under the supervision of distinguished bioinorganic chemist Prof. M. Palaniandavar, FNA, FASc, and M.Sc. from Madurai Kamaraj University (ANJA College). He was awarded a gold medal for securing the first rank in his M.Sc. He has done his Post-Doctoral Fellowship with Prof. Nadia C. Mösch-Zanetti, University of Graz, Austria. Then, he worked on iron-based PARACET MRI contrast agents at Prof. Janet Morrow’s group, University at Buffalo, SUNY, USA, with his Fulbright Scholarship grant. Also, he was an INSA-DFG visiting scientist at Prof. Peter Comba’s laboratory at Heidelberg University, Germany.
