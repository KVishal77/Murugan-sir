Research Projects

Externally funded research projects focused on bioinorganic chemistry, smart MRI contrast agents, and activation of small molecules.

Redox Activated Smart Manganese-Based Contrast Agents

Development and validation of redox-responsive manganese-based contrast agents designed for targeted molecular imaging and high-precision diagnostics of tumor environments.

Active Project • Bioinorganic Chemistry

Bioinspired Complexes for Oxygen Activation

Investigating Fe(II), Ni(II), and Cu(II) coordination complexes as functional analogues of metalloenzymes to understand catalytic mechanisms of oxygen activation and C-H bond hydroxylation.

Catalysis & Metalloenzymes

CO₂ Activation and Fixation

Designing bioinspired transition metal complexes to fix atmospheric carbon dioxide under mild conditions and convert it efficiently into valuable organic carbonates and cyclic molecules.

Green Chemistry & Sustainability

Targeted Theranostics & Nanomedicine

Bridging nanotechnology and cancer biology by engineering multifunctional nanoplatforms that combine targeted tumor imaging with therapeutic intervention and controlled drug delivery.
