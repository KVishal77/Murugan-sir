Our Research Areas

Smart MRI Probes

Designing Fe(II) macrocycles as pH-responsive PARACEST agents and Fe(III)-catecholate complexes as dual-modal T1 MRI and optical agents.

Oxygen Activation

Investigating Fe(II), Ni(II), and Cu(II) complexes as models for cysteine dioxygenase and catalytic aromatic C-H bond hydroxylation.

CO2 Activation

Developing bioinspired Cu(II) and Ni(II) complexes that fix atmospheric CO2 under mild conditions and convert it into cyclic carbonates.

Cancer Theranostics

Bridging nanotechnology and cancer biology to advance precision oncology, targeted drug delivery, and ferroptosis models.
