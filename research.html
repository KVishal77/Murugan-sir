Our Research Areas

Smart MRI Probes

Oxygen Activation

Investigating Fe(II), Ni(II), and Cu(II) complexes as models for cysteine dioxygenase and catalytic aromatic C-H bond hydroxylation[cite: 40].

CO2 Activation
