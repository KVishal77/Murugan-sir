Research Vision & Focus

Interdisciplinary approaches bridging bioinorganic chemistry, spectroscopy, and biological validation

Smart MRI & Optical Probes

Designing tailored metal complexes as smart probes to target tumours, imaging and quantifying reactive oxygen species (ROS), reactive nitrogen species (RNS), and abnormal redox environments within cancer cells for early diagnostics.

Chemical Biology & Therapeutics

Translating bioinspired coordination complexes toward clinical evaluation as novel therapeutic and diagnostic agents targeting cancer and related physiological disorders.

Bioinspired Catalysis

Synthesizing structural and functional analogues of metalloenzymes to activate small molecules such as dioxygen ($O_2$) and carbon dioxide ($CO_2$) under mild catalytic conditions.
