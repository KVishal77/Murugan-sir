Research Thrust Areas

Chemists and biologists working together on health problems, combining design, synthesis, and biological validation.

Bioinorganic Chemistry

Smart MRI Probes

Fe(II) macrocycles as pH-responsive PARACEST agents, and Fe(III)-catecholate complexes as dual-modal T1 MRI and optical agents for nitric oxide and acidic tumour pH. Biotin conjugation raises agent delivery to tumours. Mn(II/III) complexes serve as redox-responsive agents sensitive to L-cysteine.

Oxygen Activation

Fe(II), Ni(II), and Cu(II) complexes as models for cysteine dioxygenase, diketone dioxygenase, and copper monooxygenases, acting as catalysts for aromatic C-H bond hydroxylation, investigated via spectroscopy and computation.

CO₂ Activation

Bioinspired Cu(II) and Ni(II) complexes fixing atmospheric CO₂ under mild conditions to convert it into cyclic carbonates and carboxylic acids, validated with crystal structures of CO₂-derived intermediates.

Chemical Biology

Nanomedicine for Targeted Therapy

Advancing precision oncology via nanomedicine-based approaches for targeted cancer therapy, incorporating protein nanocages, engineered nanoparticles, liposomes, and hybrid nanostructures to enhance selectivity while minimizing systemic toxicity.

Ferroptosis & Cancer Models

Focusing on metal-dependent regulated cell death, specifically ferroptosis and its therapeutic exploitation against cervical, breast, and colorectal cancer models through cellular viability, cytotoxicity, gene regulation, and molecular pathways.

Cancer Theranostics

Integrating diagnostic imaging with therapeutic interventions using multifunctional molecules and nanoplatforms for image-guided cancer intervention, bridging nanotechnology and cancer biology.
