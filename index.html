Welcome to the RMM Group!

Department of Chemistry, BioScience & Biomedical Engineering IIT Bhilai

Dr. Ramasamy Mayilmurugan

Associate Professor

Department of Chemistry, IIT Bhilai

Our group focuses on "interdisciplinary research" that involves the design and synthesis of carefully tailored molecules to act as smart MRI and optical probes to target tumours and understand their mechanism. Our research aims to use biologically inspired metal complexes as probes to understand complex biochemical processes in tumours. We are using these bioinspired imaging probes and our chemistry knowledge to image and quantify reactive species produced via the redox process within tumor cells.

We are synthesizing a library of architecturally designed bioinspired coordination complexes as synthetic analogues of metalloenzymes or as bioinspired catalysts for activating small molecules such as dioxygen and carbon dioxide. We named our group "Bioinorganic Chemistry and Chemical Biology," where chemists and biologists work together to address health issues.

Research

Chemists and biologists working together on health problems, combining design, synthesis and biological validation.

Bioinorganic chemistry

🧪

Smart MRI probes

Fe(II) macrocycles as pH-responsive PARACEST agents, and Fe(III)-catecholate complexes as dual-modal T1 MRI and optical agents for nitric oxide and acidic tumour pH.

🔬

Oxygen activation

Fe(II), Ni(II) and Cu(II) complexes as models for cysteine dioxygenase, diketone dioxygenase and copper monooxygenases, studied with spectroscopy.

⚛️

CO₂ activation

Bioinspired Cu(II) and Ni(II) complexes that fix atmospheric CO₂ under mild conditions and convert it into cyclic carbonates and carboxylic acids.

Chemical biology

💊

Nanomedicine for targeted therapy

Advancing precision oncology through innovative nanomedicine-based approaches, protein nanocages, engineered nanoparticles, and liposomes.

🧬

Ferroptosis and cancer models

Metal-dependent regulated cell death, particularly ferroptosis, and its therapeutic exploitation against cervical, breast, and colorectal cancer models.

🎯

Cancer theranostics

Multifunctional molecules and nanoplatforms integrating diagnostic imaging with therapeutic applications for image-guided intervention.
